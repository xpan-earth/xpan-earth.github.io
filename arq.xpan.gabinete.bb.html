gabinete.bb

cabinet developed for beibi beauty as part of [19.4300_99.1747].

the system configures six stations for product display and use through a linear structure that integrates xpan’s poros system as its mounting mechanism. anchoring points distributed along the structure allow different devices to be incorporated, positioned and exchanged without depending on a fixed configuration.

six circular mirrors and six individual shelves are mounted on this infrastructure as independent devices connected to the system, organizing a simultaneous sequence of product, support surface and reflection. its components can be removed, replaced or reconfigured.

the structure measures approximately 160 × 33 × 140 cm and is built from square carbon-steel bars, connection plates and a ground finish. the devices incorporate machined aluminum, brushed aluminum flat bar and 20 cm diameter mirrors.
